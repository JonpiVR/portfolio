Research project · Bachelor thesis · DHBW Karlsruhe · 2026

Implementing and measuring a multi-agent system with RAG and MCP for enterprise architects

Enterprise architects at Netze BW spend a lot of time searching scattered internal documentation. In my bachelor thesis I designed and built a multi-agent system that answers their questions from this knowledge, and defined how to measure whether its answers can be trusted.

Question
Can a multi-agent system that combines retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) give enterprise architects reliable answers from internal architecture knowledge, and how can that quality be measured?
Method
Implementation of a multi-agent system on Azure AI Foundry: specialised agents retrieve relevant documents via RAG and access tools and data sources through MCP. The system was then evaluated against defined quality criteria for its answers.
Result
A working prototype for Netze BW, plus a measurement approach for evaluating answer quality of agent-based RAG systems in an enterprise setting.
Grade
Bachelor completed with an overall GPA of 2.0
